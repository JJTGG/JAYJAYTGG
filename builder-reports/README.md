# Builder Reports

A private, chronological archive of JAYJAYTGG's operating reports. Reports are generated and presented in ChatGPT first; the approved weekly and monthly reports are then archived here as completed records.

The archive is organised by reporting cadence. Each published report preserves the generated report's substantive content, reporting period, cutoff and evidence limitations.

Publishing is secondary to report generation: a GitBook failure does not invalidate a report produced in ChatGPT.

## Collections

* [Daily Reports](daily-reports.md) — selected daily operating records.
* [Weekly Reports](weekly-reports.md) — completed weekly reviews, published after they appear in ChatGPT.
* [Monthly Reports](monthly-reports.md) — completed monthly reviews, published after they appear in ChatGPT.
